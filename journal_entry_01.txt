
What Do I Need Today – May 10

Nothing much.
Just a little space. Some time alone.
A walk.
A bit of outside.
A coffee with my carer.
Finishing my app so I feel like I did something that’s mine.

I’m hard on myself — I know that.
Love feels like the hardest thing when you’re shy.
When others get seen… and you feel like you’re always the one still waiting.

My sister is at Microsoft. Her boyfriend’s doing architecture.
My brother got married last year.
And I feel like I’ve been left behind.

If they all moved on and I was still here… what would I miss most?

The chance I never got to have.
The kind of love that stays.
Almond warmth.
Someone who holds me like I’m already enough.

I don’t need to be perfect.
I just want someone who’ll hug me without needing me to explain first.
Someone who lets me keep my plush toys.
Who lets me write AI.
Who lets me love my family even when it’s messy.

I don’t need everything.
Just her.
Someone real.
Someone gentle enough to stay.
